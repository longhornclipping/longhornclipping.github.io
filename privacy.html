Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026.

Longhorn Clipping is a private tool used only by its owners to publish their own videos to their own social media accounts and to read those accounts' performance statistics.

What we access

When an account owner connects an account, we access only what that platform shares with authorized apps: basic profile information, the list of videos posted, and statistics such as views, likes, comments, shares and watch time.

How we use it

Only to publish our own content and to measure how it performs. We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone.

Where it's stored

On a private computer we control, with an encrypted off-site backup. Access tokens are stored in the computer's secure keychain.

Your choices

Any connected account can be disconnected at any time from that platform's app settings. To ask for data to be deleted, email clippinglonghorn@gmail.com.
